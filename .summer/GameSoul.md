Vision

A fast-paced 5v5 street football game for mobile that captures the intensity and joy of competitive small-sided play. Your game puts players in control of a single footballer in a compact stadium, delivering quick matches filled with tactical passing, explosive shots, and the thrill of real-time competition.

Genre and Inspirations

You're creating a sports action game with arcade sensibilities. The vibrant, arcade-like energy means bright colors, snappy feedback, and a game that feels alive and reactive. The clean, modern aesthetic keeps the interface uncluttered and the action readable. The competitive, intense vibe runs through everything: match timers, scoreboards, and AI opponents that put pressure on the player moment by moment.

Look and Feel

Imagine a compact stadium bathed in vivid, punchy colors. The players are simple, original 3D figures in clean-lined kits. The ball moves with weight and physics, catching light as it arcs across the pitch. UI elements are minimal and modern: a bold scoreboard, a timer counting down, clean team selection screens. Everything feels responsive and immediate, with none of the clutter of licensed sports games. The overall impression is energetic, accessible, and intensely focused on the match itself.

Core Mechanics

The player controls one footballer using touch controls on mobile, managing movement, passing, shooting, and sprinting. Ball possession is real and tactile: tap to move, swipe to pass, hold to charge a shot. Other players on your team follow AI patterns, while the goalkeeper reacts to shots. Tackles and positioning matter. The match flows in real time toward a final whistle. Simple goal detection and a clear scoring system keep the stakes high and the feedback instant.

What Makes It Special

Your game strips away the bloat of big-budget sports sims and focuses on the pure mechanics of small-sided football: quick decisions, direct action, constant pressure. By keeping teams small (5v5) and the stadium compact, you create an intense, arcade-like rhythm where every touch matters. The mobile-first design and commitment to placeholder art means you're building a game that's fun to play, not pretty to look at first. That's the right priority.
